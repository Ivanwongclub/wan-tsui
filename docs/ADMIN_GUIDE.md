# 環翠綜合醫務中心 — 網站管理指南
# Wan Tsui Integrated Medical Centre — Website Admin Guide

---

## 1. 登入 / Signing in

在瀏覽器輸入網址後加上 `/admin`，例如 `www.wt-medical.com/admin`。輸入診所的電郵地址及密碼，按「Sign in」。第一次登入後，請盡快更改密碼，並只讓需要更新網站的同事知道。

Go to your website address followed by `/admin`, for example `www.wt-medical.com/admin`. Enter the clinic email address and password, then press Sign in. After your first sign-in, please change the password, and share it only with staff who need to update the website.

---

## 2. 修改文字 / Editing text

按左邊的「Content」。頁面分成多個部分（例如診所資料、醫生、服務）。按一下部分的標題把它打開，找到要修改的項目，在「繁中」方格輸入中文，在「English」方格輸入英文，然後按該部分的「Save」。

兩種語言分開儲存：只改中文，英文版網站不會受影響；只改英文，中文版網站也不會受影響。

Press Content on the left. The page is divided into sections (clinic details, doctors, services, and so on). Click a section title to open it, find the item you want to change, type the Chinese wording in the 繁中 box and the English wording in the English box, then press Save in that section. Each language is saved separately, so changing one does not affect the other.

---

## 3. 「Reset」的作用 / What Reset does

按「Reset」會把該部分回復到網站原本的文字。

如果你把方格清空再儲存，網站上的文字**不會**變成空白 — 它會自動回復到原本的文字。

Reset restores the original wording for that section. If you clear a box and save, the website does **not** go blank — it simply returns to the original wording.

---

## 4. 醫生當值時間表 / The doctor duty timetable

按左邊的「Schedule」。表內固定七行，由星期一至星期日。在「上午」「下午」的方格輸入當值醫生的姓名（中文及英文各一格）。如果該節休息，請開啟「休息」開關，該節的方格便會停用。開關不會刪除已輸入的名字。改好整個表之後，按上方的「Save」一次過儲存。

Press Schedule on the left. The table always has seven rows, Monday to Sunday. Type the duty doctor's name for the morning and afternoon sessions, in Chinese and in English. If a session is closed, switch on the 休息 (closed) switch; the boxes for that session are then greyed out. The switch never deletes the names you typed. When the whole table is correct, press Save once at the top.

---

## 5. 更換相片 / Replacing images

按左邊的「Images」。每張相片有一張卡。按「Replace」，選擇一個 JPG、PNG 或 WebP 檔案，檔案要小於 5MB。上載後新相片會立即顯示在網站上。按「Restore default」可以換回原本的相片。

Press Images on the left. Each picture has its own card. Press Replace and choose a JPG, PNG or WebP file smaller than 5MB. The new picture appears on the website straight away. Press Restore default to put the original picture back.

---

## 6. 請注意 / Please note

所有改動按下「Save」後會即時在網站顯示，無需等待。

診所的電話號碼、電郵地址及地址不能在這裡修改。如需更改，請聯絡 Tune Bright。

All changes appear on the website immediately after you press Save. The clinic's phone number, email address and address of record cannot be changed here — please contact Tune Bright for those.
